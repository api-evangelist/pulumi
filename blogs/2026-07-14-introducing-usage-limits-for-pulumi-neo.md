Pulumi Neo is an AI agent that takes on real infrastructure work, and it’s natural to want to hand it more and more. Usage limits give you control so you can do exactly that: set a monthly dollar limit, and Neo pauses when your organization reaches it. How usage limits work Your organization limit is a single monthly dollar amount covering all Neo usage across the org.
