Today we’re launching Discovered Stacks : Pulumi Cloud now models your AWS CloudFormation stacks and Azure Resource Manager deployments as stacks, right alongside your Pulumi IaC stacks. And when you’re ready to bring them under Pulumi management, migration is built in, with every resource tracked until the code provably matches the cloud. Why: your infrastructure doesn’t live in one tool Almost nobody’s cloud estate is a single technology.
